MBM GUIDE

How MBM works

1. Set up the property

Add one property, its location, then its units. This is the master record used everywhere else.

2. Record occupancy

Link a tenant and contract to an existing unit. Expiry dates automatically feed renewals, control and reports.

3. Create invoices and record money

Create an invoice against the agreement, record payments, allocate receipts and track one municipal account per property.

4. Deliver an invoice

Open the invoice in Finance, save it as a PDF, then use Send to prepare WhatsApp, email or a copied message. Review, attach and send manually; MBM records the prepared action.

5. Manage service

Log a work order or inspection against the property/unit. It appears in the control queue and reporting.

6. Review and act

Use Today for priorities, Control Centre for deadlines/documents and Insights for management reporting. Search from the top to find any record.

Smart Assistant is private, rule-based help running inside this browser. It does not send your property records to an AI provider.
