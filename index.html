Corporate property architecture

  1. Portfolio & Assets maintains the approved property inventory, ERF records, status and occupancy baseline.
  2. Leasing & Contracts links tenants, units, rent, contract expiry, renewal readiness and legal agreement status.
  3. Revenue & Collections captures recurring rent receipts, arrears, billing history and WhatsApp receipt delivery.
  4. Municipal Bill holds one current municipal account per property for operating-cost visibility.
  5. Compliance & Assurance exposes missing contracts, lease expiry and overdue municipal exceptions for management action.
  6. Corporate Reports provides collection, arrears, portfolio and future API-ready reporting.
  7. Cloud, API & Integrations keeps owner-only data secure and provides a future server-side route for municipality, accounting and document integrations.
